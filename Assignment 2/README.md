# a short description

this dataset contains sales data for Nike products. It includes information about product categories, pricing, discounts, sales channels, and regional performance. The dataset allows us to analyze sales patterns, revenue generation, and profitability across different products.

# Nike_Sales_Uncleaned.csv Info:

1- Order_ID: Unique identifier for each sales order.
2- Gender_Category:(Men, Women, Kids).
3- Product_Line: The category of the product such as training, running, or soccer.
4- Product_Name: The name of the Nike product.
5- Size: The size of the product purchased.
6- Units_Sold: The number of units sold in the order.
7- MRP: Maximum Retail Price of the product.
8- Discount_Applied: The discount amount applied to the product.
9- Revenue: Total revenue generated from the sale.
10- Order_Date: The date when the order was placed.
11- Sales_Channel: The platform where the product was sold (Online or Retail).
12- Region: The geographical region where the sale occurred.
13- Profit: The profit obtained from the sale after accounting for costs and discounts.
